# Staff payment schedule recorded instant

Status: contracted for `ADMIN-PAYMENT-SCHEDULE-CREATED` / `FZ-REQ-ADMIN-063`.

## Surface

A staff payment schedule shows its recorded instant in Polish UTC words.
A missing or unreadable instant rejects the schedule list. The raw
timestamp is not shown.

## Out of scope

- A new timestamp field or a stored clock.
- Changing a stored minor amount, a provider, or a charge.
- DNS, Cloudflare, deploy, and live customer data.
