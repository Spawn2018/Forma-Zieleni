# Staff payment schedule order

Status: contracted for `ADMIN-PAYMENT-SCHEDULE-ORDER` / `FZ-REQ-ADMIN-064`.

## Surface

The staff payment schedule list orders schedules by the earlier recorded
instant. The same instant stays in id order. Every schedule stays on the
list.

## Out of scope

- A new timestamp field or a stored sort.
- Changing a stored minor amount, a provider, or a charge.
- DNS, Cloudflare, deploy, and live customer data.
