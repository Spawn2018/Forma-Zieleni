# Staff site recorded instant

Status: contracted for `ADMIN-SITE-CREATED` / `FZ-REQ-ADMIN-050`.

## Surface

A staff site record shows the instant it was recorded, in Polish UTC
words. An unreadable or missing instant rejects the list. The raw
timestamp is not shown.

## Out of scope

- A new timestamp field or a stored clock.
- Finding codes, prices, payment, and signing.
- DNS, Cloudflare, deploy, and live customer data.
