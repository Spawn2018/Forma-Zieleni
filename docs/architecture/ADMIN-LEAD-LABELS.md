# Staff lead status labels

Status: contracted for `ADMIN-LEAD-LABELS` / `FZ-REQ-ADMIN-053`.

## Surface

A staff lead shows its status in Polish:

- `received` — przyjęty
- `site_analysis` — analiza terenu
- `qualified` — zakwalifikowany
- `consultation_ready` — gotowy do konsultacji
- `unqualified` — niezakwalifikowany

An unknown status token rejects the list. The raw token is not shown.

## Out of scope

- A new lead status, qualification label, price, or signature.
- DNS, Cloudflare, deploy, and live customer data.
