# Staff installment order

Status: contracted for `ADMIN-PAYMENT-INSTALLMENT-ORDER` / `FZ-REQ-ADMIN-061`.

## Surface

A staff payment schedule lists installments by the lower sequence first.
The same sequence stays in id order. Every installment stays on the list.

## Out of scope

- A new sequence field or a stored sort.
- Changing a stored minor amount, a provider, or a charge.
- DNS, Cloudflare, deploy, and live customer data.
