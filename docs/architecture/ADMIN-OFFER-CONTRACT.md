# Staff offer contracts

Status: contracted for `ADMIN-OFFER-CONTRACT` / `FZ-REQ-ADMIN-047`.

## Surface

A staff offer names the contracts already loaded for that offer. One
contract is `Umowa: <id>`. Several contracts are `Umowy: <id>, <id>` in
id order. Another offer’s contract stays off the row. A failed or
forbidden contract read stays a failed or forbidden read.

## Out of scope

- A new contract, price, payment, or signature.
- DNS, Cloudflare, deploy, and live customer data.
