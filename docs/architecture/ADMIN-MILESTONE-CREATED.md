# Staff milestone recorded instant

Status: contracted for `ADMIN-MILESTONE-CREATED` / `FZ-REQ-ADMIN-055`.

## Surface

A staff milestone shows the instant it was recorded, in Polish UTC
words. An unreadable or missing instant rejects the list. The raw
timestamp is not shown.

## Out of scope

- A new timestamp field or a stored clock.
- Due-date edits, status changes, prices, payment, and signing.
- DNS, Cloudflare, deploy, and live customer data.
