# Staff decision-log order

Status: contracted for `ADMIN-DECISION-LOG-ORDER` / `FZ-REQ-ADMIN-045`.

## Surface

The staff decision log orders entries by the earlier recorded instant.
The same instant stays in id order. Every entry stays on the list.

## Out of scope

- A new timestamp field or a stored sort.
- Price, payment, signing, and the recording actor id.
- A portal read of the decision log.
- DNS, Cloudflare, deploy, and live customer data.
