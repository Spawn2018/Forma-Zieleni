# performance-engineering
Read docs/engineering/PERFORMANCE-ENGINEERING.md. Establish a baseline and workload before optimization. Find the dominant bottleneck with evidence. Change one material variable at a time where practical. Re-measure. Record regressions and trade-offs. Never invent an SLO or optimize a synthetic metric at the expense of user experience.
