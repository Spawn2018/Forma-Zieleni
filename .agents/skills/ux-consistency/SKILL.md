---
name: ux-consistency
description: Preserve Forma Zieleni lifecycle, roles and product behavior when implementing flows or screens.
---
Read `docs/ux/UX-PRODUCT-CANON.md`, Product Canon and relevant domain/contracts before changing a flow.

Map screen → user → job → domain state → allowed actions → next action → error/empty/loading/degraded states. Reuse known data and preserve progress. Do not invent major roles, lifecycle stages, navigation models or business workflows. Material UX changes are DECISION.
