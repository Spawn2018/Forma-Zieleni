---
name: database-change-safety
description: Review a database/schema/query change for correctness, migration safety and measured performance.
---
Check domain invariants, constraints, nullability, keys, indexes based on workload, transaction boundaries, concurrency, query plans, pagination/bounds, rollback/roll-forward strategy, backup/restore implications, authorization and PII. Avoid speculative denormalization or indexing. Require evidence for performance claims.
